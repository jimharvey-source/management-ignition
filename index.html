The problem

The knowledge was never the problem.

Most leaders have been through the training. They have read the books, sat through the workshops, understood the frameworks. The four conditions make sense. The skills are clear. And yet the gap between knowing and doing is where most leadership development budgets are wasted.

A training course teaches the skill. It does not sit with you three months later, up at eight on a Tuesday morning, when you need to give feedback to someone who is not delivering, or delegate a significant piece of work to someone who is capable but lacking in confidence.

The gap between what a manager wants to do for their team, and what they have time to do, grows every day. That is where good managers start to feel they are failing people who deserve better.

Most leaders have the knowledge. What they have never had is a practical co-pilot at their fingertips that puts that knowledge to work in the moment they need it. Not a course. Not a coach on a retainer. Something that is there at eight on a Tuesday morning, when the conversation cannot wait.

The suite

Five conversations. Five tools.
One record.

Each tool handles one of the conversations that decide whether a team performs. Together they build the person record, a running history of every goal set, every delegation made, every piece of feedback given, every coaching session, and every action agreed.

Goal Ignite
Builds Purpose
Sharpens the goal before you set it. Tests clarity, measurability and relevance, connects the goal to the person's development, and builds in the review points most goal conversations skip. You get a goal statement to share and coaching notes on how to build genuine commitment.
Delegate Ignite
Builds the Plan
Assesses skill and confidence, recommends the right level of freedom from ten calibrated levels, and generates a full briefing note. Tells you how often to check in and adds the review to your calendar. Plus coaching notes on pitching the challenge correctly.
Feedback Ignite
Holds the People standard
Turns rough notes into feedback the person can act on. If your notes are too vague, it asks the right questions first. Choose Empathetic or Direct. You get a feedback note to share and coaching notes on how to open, how to hold the difficult moment, and how to close with commitment.
Coach Ignite
Builds Pride, and People confidence
Prepares the development conversation. Builds a GROW-structured coaching brief, a conversation guide with the right questions, and a development summary to share afterwards. Structure without a script.
Meeting Ignite
Holds the four together
Builds an outcome-based agenda before, captures actions with owners and deadlines after. A facilitation guide for you, a clean actions summary for everyone else. The time your team already spends together starts producing results.

Your data

What we store, where it lives,
and who can see it.

Every note you write about a team member is personal data about a real person. We treat it that way.

Stored in Stockholm
Your data is stored and processed in Europe. It does not travel to a server outside the EEA.
Your records are yours
Every record is owned by the account that created it. The database enforces this at row level, so a defect in the application cannot expose another manager's records.
Admins cannot read your notes
If your organisation buys seats, the administrator sees seat usage and billing. They cannot read your notes or anything you have written about your team.
Export or delete, any time
Export any person record and everything attached to it in one action. Delete it just as easily. Deletion is permanent and takes everything attached to them with it.

We say the admin point plainly because it matters. Managers write honestly when they know their notes will not be read by someone else and used against them, or against the person they are writing about.
